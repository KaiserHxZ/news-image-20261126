## The five stories

1. **Cairo tanker fire: at least 31 killed.** A fuel tanker crashed and caught fire on Cairo's Ring Road in the Warraq district of Giza. Egypt's health ministry put the toll at at least 31 dead and 21 injured, described as preliminary.
2. **US proposes tariffs on 14 trading partners.** The US Trade Representative proposed new Section 301 tariffs on 14 economies, including China, the EU, Japan and India. The duties are proposals only, subject to a comment period and a hearing scheduled for January 2027; they had not taken effect.
3. **Houthis claim Ras Tanura refinery hit.** Yemen's Houthis said they attacked Saudi Arabia's largest refinery, Ras Tanura, and Riyadh's financial district. The Saudi-led coalition said air defences intercepted four drones over Ras Tanura and did not confirm the refinery was struck; Reuters reported a source confirming an attack and satellite images showing smoke.
4. **First foreign astronaut aboard Tiangong.** Pakistani payload specialist Muhammad Zeeshan Ali flew aboard China's Shenzhou-24 and became the first foreign astronaut to board the Tiangong space station, and the first Pakistani to reach orbit.
5. **Solomon Islands–Australia pact.** Solomon Islands and Australia signed a treaty in Canberra making Australia the Solomon Islands' primary security partner, with the treaty affirming there will be no foreign military or security base on Solomon Islands soil. Reporting described the deal as a setback for China's regional ambitions; Prime Minister Matthew Wale declined to comment on the status of the earlier security pact with China, so the image makes no claim about that pact.
